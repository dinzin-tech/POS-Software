1. Information We Collect

When you use our website, place takeaway orders, request home delivery, or make dine-in table reservations, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact Data: Full name, 10-digit mobile phone number.
  • Delivery Address: Street, building, and landmarks in Nelamangala.
  • Order Items: Selected dishes, spice preference, and notes.
  • Dine-In Info: Table number and guest count.

2. Purpose of Information Processing

We process data strictly to prepare fresh orders, execute takeaway/delivery, print kitchen tickets (KOT), issue statutory tax invoices (GSTIN: 29CVIPR3058J1ZY), and communicate status via WhatsApp.

3. Direct WhatsApp Channel

Order updates and digital bill receipts are sent directly through your provided WhatsApp phone number. We do not use your phone number for unsolicited marketing or automated robocalls.

4. Non-Disclosure & Security

CHANDU ENTERPRISES does not sell, rent, or trade customer information to third-party advertising brokers. Data is accessed only by store operators and delivery personnel fulfilling your order.
